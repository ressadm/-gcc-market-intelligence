## 1. Corporate structure and people

### Takeaway
HUMAIN is organised in four layers (data centres, infrastructure/cloud, data and models, applications) plus an investment activity; it is chaired by the Crown Prince and run by CEO Tareq Amin with a small named executive team. No board list, legal-entity chart or official headcount is published; third-party directories put headcount at roughly 1,000–1,100.

### Cited Findings
- Four business layers named at launch: Infrastructure (data centres), Cloud, Data & Models, Applications; product areas also listed as sovereign cloud, AI devices and "AI innovation hubs". Launch date 12 May 2025. — [Middle East AI News, 12 May 2025, updated 17 May 2025](https://www.middleeastainews.com/p/saudi-arabia-humain-ai-powerhouse) (S)
- Executive team as listed at launch (the article says the list was obtained through HUMAIN's own AI assistant and may need verification): Tareq Amin (CEO); Dr Yaser Al-Onaizan (Deputy CEO and President, Data & AI Models); Samih Elhage (Chief Financial & Operations Officer); Steve Plimsoll (Chief Strategy Officer, acting CMO); Jeffrey Thomas (President, AI Data Centers); Saeed Dobas (President, AI Infrastructure & Cloud); George Nazi (President, AI Applications & Solutions); Dr Khalid Alodhaibi (CTO); Ali Siddiqui (CTO, AI Infrastructure & Cloud). — [Middle East AI News](https://www.middleeastainews.com/p/saudi-arabia-humain-ai-powerhouse) (S)

### Inferences
- HUMAIN is roughly 18 months old with about 1,000 staff, much of it inherited from SCAI/SDAIA/Aramco units; G42 by contrast is a multi-company group founded in 2018. The comparison is between a new consolidated state vehicle and an established holding group.
- Three launch-era business-unit presidents are missing from the later directory listing. This may reflect an incomplete directory rather than departures; it should not be reported as turnover without confirmation.

### Gaps
- Board members other than the chair: not published in any source found.
- Legal entities/subsidiaries (other than SCAI and the unnamed AMD–Cisco JV): not found. Whether SCAI's stake in the Alibaba-linked SCCC came across with it: unconfirmed.
- Official headcount, Saudisation ratio, office addresses: not disclosed.
- Notable departures: a targeted search returned nothing relevant; I found no reliable report of any named executive leaving.
- Whether Aramco Digital as an entity, and the Groq Dammam cluster, moved to HUMAIN at closing: still unconfirmed by any primary source.

## 2. Ownership and capital

### Takeaway
Since 1 November 2026 HUMAIN is 51% PIF / 49% Aramco, with Aramco putting in about SAR 9.9bn (US$2.6bn) of cash during 2026 plus unnamed assets; there are no other shareholders and no published valuation. Outside capital so far consists of state-linked facilities (National Infrastructure Fund, up to SAR 2.5bn signed November 2026) and announced partner commitments; a Goldman Sachs-advised financing of at least SAR 20bn was reported in May 2026 but no closing has been reported.

### Cited Findings
- **[ACTUAL]** Closing announced 1 Nov 2026 (Dhahran): Aramco, through a wholly owned subsidiary, holds 49%; PIF 51%; cash of about SAR 9.9bn (US$2.6bn) contributed "in 2026" in addition to AI, compute and digital assets; PIF contributes "AI assets, capabilities, and talent"; no valuation; no other shareholder. Quotes from Yazeed Al-Humied (PIF) and Amin Nasser (Aramco), who cited US$11.3bn of "Technology Realized Value" at Aramco since 2023. — [Aramco press release, 1 Nov 2026](https://www.aramco.com/en/news-media/news/2026/pif-and-aramco-close-humain-transaction-to-accelerate-humains-global-growth) (P); same text carried by [Saudi Press Agency](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2715118) (P, search summary)
- The release describes the cash as going into HUMAIN and mentions no payment to PIF (i.e. primary capital, not a secondary sale); one commentary notes Aramco's public shareholders cannot judge the price without a valuation. — [Universal Asset Owners commentary](https://www.universalassetowners.com/newsroom/aramco-closes-49-percent-humain-pif-ai-champion/) (search summary)
- One Arabic-language report gave Aramco's cash as "about US$26bn"; this conflicts with the primary release (US$2.6bn) and should be treated as an error. — search summary of Arabic coverage; primary figure per [Aramco](https://www.aramco.com/en/news-media/news/2026/pif-and-aramco-close-humain-transaction-to-accelerate-humains-global-growth) (P)
- **[TARGET]** Funding mix: 30% shareholder / 70% third-party; "not there yet"; offtake contracts with large tech companies used to raise bank debt; stated intent to reduce reliance on PIF and open to global investors. — [Semafor, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.semafor.com/article/09/04/2026/the-switzerland-of-ai-saudi-arabias-big-ai-bet) (S)
- **[MoU]** 21–22 Jan 2026 (Davos): framework with the National Infrastructure Fund ("Infra") for up to SAR 4.5bn (US$1.2bn), described as non-binding. — [Enterprise AM, 11 Nov 2026](https://enterpriseam.com/ksa/2026/11/11/humains-getting-a-big-cash-infusion-from-the-saudi-national-infrastructure-fund/) (S)
- **[COMMITTED, "up to" amounts]** 8–10 Nov 2026: two Infra financing agreements totalling up to SAR 2.5bn (US$667m): up to SAR 1.1bn (US$293m) for GPUs covering about 25MW of IT load; up to SAR 1.4bn (US$373m) for substations/transmission/grid links sized to support up to 1GW of IT capacity. First deals under Infra's "AI Data Center Financing Program" and first tranche of the January framework. — [Enterprise AM, 11 Nov 2026](https://enterpriseam.com/ksa/2026/11/11/humains-getting-a-big-cash-infusion-from-the-saudi-national-infrastructure-fund/) (S); [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/humain-raises-667m-for-ai-buildout-in-saudi-arabia/), [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/eye-on-ai/humain-infra-sign-667m-financing-deals-to-expand-saudi-ai-infrastructure-3010704), [Asharq Al-Awsat (Arabic)](https://aawsat.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF/5339281-%D9%87%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%B5%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%AA%D9%85%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A8%D9%80667-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%B9-%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A) (search summary; signing dated 8 or 9 Nov depending on source)
- **[Reported, not closed]** 19 May 2026 (Reuters, two unnamed sources): HUMAIN hired Goldman Sachs to advise on financing of at least SAR 20bn (US$5.33bn) for data centres and GPUs for 2GW of capacity in the Riyadh area, "around a third of its target by 2034"; HUMAIN and Goldman declined to comment. — [Reuters via US News](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-05-19/saudi-arabias-humain-picks-goldman-sachs-to-advise-on-data-centre-financing-sources-say); [Reuters via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/saudi-arabias-humain-picks-goldman-141120422.html) (search summary)

- **[TARGET]** Investment vehicles: US$10bn venture fund announced by Aug 2025 (alongside "US$23bn for strategic technology partnerships"); in Sept 2026 Amin said it could exceed US$10bn and would launch "this year"; Saudi-focused vehicle "HUMAIN Limitless"; initial US$2.5bn data-centre investment vehicle to bring in non-PIF capital. — [CNBC, 27 Aug 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/27/saudi-arabia-wants-to-be-worlds-third-largest-ai-provider-humain.html) (S); [FWDstart, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.fwdstart.me/p/humain-starts-ipo-preparations-16-months-after-launch-with-revenues-still-undisclosed) (S)
- **[TARGET]** IPO: dual listing (Saudi Exchange and Nasdaq) within three to four years of Oct 2025 (about 2029); no banks appointed, no size, valuation or date as of Sept 2026. — [FWDstart, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.fwdstart.me/p/humain-starts-ipo-preparations-16-months-after-launch-with-revenues-still-undisclosed) (S)

### Inferences
- Every dollar of capital evidenced to date is Saudi-state-linked (PIF, Aramco, Infra). The 70% third-party target is not yet met by Amin's own account, and the largest reported external raise (Goldman, SAR 20bn) had no reported close by mid-November 2026.
- The Infra GPU facility implies roughly SAR 44m (about US$11.7m) per MW of IT load for chips (SAR 1.1bn / 25MW), in line with Amin's US$11–12m/MW industry figure quoted by Semafor. This is my arithmetic, not a disclosed unit cost.
- The Infra GPU tranche (25MW) is small relative to gigawatt targets; the power tranche (grid for up to 1GW) is the more strategic piece.

### Gaps
- Valuation, PIF's cumulative cash injection, value attributed to Aramco's and PIF's non-cash contributions: undisclosed.
- Whether the Blackstone/AirTrunk US$3bn is Blackstone capital, HUMAIN capital or joint project cost: sources conflict; not resolved.
- Whether the VC fund has had a first close; any LP names: not found.
- Debt outstanding and any bank facility other than Infra: not found.

## 3. Infrastructure reality check

### Takeaway
Publicly evidenced live capacity remains tiny against the targets: 1.1MW of Nvidia-based cloud (late August 2026), an unquantified AMD MI355X deployment, 1,024 Qualcomm AI100 accelerators (February 2026) and a Groq inference cluster in Dammam whose ownership by HUMAIN is unconfirmed. No HUMAIN site has a published energisation record; an independent July 2026 audit scored HUMAIN's Nvidia and AMD programmes as "announced" with energised MW "not publicly established". The US authorised up to 35,000 GB300-equivalents in November 2025; the number delivered is undisclosed. Saudi Arabia did not receive the rule-based treatment the UAE obtained in July 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Targets**
- **[TARGET, history]** Aug 2025: 50MW built by Q4 2025 then 50MW per quarter; 1.9GW by 2030; 6GW by 2034; two campuses with 11 data centres of 200MW each. — [CNBC, 27 Aug 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/27/saudi-arabia-wants-to-be-worlds-third-largest-ai-provider-humain.html) (S). Current statement: 3GW by 2030. — [Aramco, 1 Nov 2026](https://www.aramco.com/en/news-media/news/2026/pif-and-aramco-close-humain-transaction-to-accelerate-humains-global-growth) (P)
- The Aug 2025 cadence (50MW by Q4 2025, +50MW per quarter) would imply about 250MW by Q4 2026; disclosed live capacity is far below this (see below). — arithmetic from [CNBC, 27 Aug 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/27/saudi-arabia-wants-to-be-worlds-third-largest-ai-provider-humain.html)

## 7. Strategy and positioning

### Takeaway
HUMAIN's stated ambition is to make Saudi Arabia the third-largest AI (infrastructure) provider after the US and China and "one of the leading global exporters of AI", by selling cheap, energy-advantaged compute and a sovereign full stack, while positioning as a neutral "Switzerland" for open-weight models. It differs from G42 in being infrastructure- and offtake-led, wholly state-owned, without a US strategic shareholder, and without a published export-control compliance framework equivalent to G42's.
