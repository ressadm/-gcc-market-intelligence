## 1. Corporate structure and people

### Takeaway
G42 is a holding company controlled, through Royal Group, by Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed; it has six named directors, about ten operating companies of which only two (Presight, Space42) are listed and publish accounts, and no reliable published headcount. The same small group of people (Tahnoon, Khaldoon Al Mubarak, Peng Xiao, Jassem Al Zaabi) sits across G42, MGX, Mubadala and the Abu Dhabi AI council.

## 3. Infrastructure reality check

### Takeaway
Operating capacity is far smaller than headline plans: Khazna said in August 2026 it had about 300 MW operating in the UAE and ~370 MW under construction/commissioning; no commissioning notice for Stargate UAE's first 200 MW had been published by mid-November 2026 (schedule moved from "Q3 2026" to "2026"/Q4). The 5 GW campus is reportedly being redrawn into dispersed, hardened sites. G42's verified compute abroad is modest (60 MW Buffalo, 20 MW Minneapolis lease) against announced gigawatt ambitions.

## 8. Strategy and positioning (and how the model differs from HUMAIN's)

### Takeaway
G42 positions itself as the trusted, US-aligned intermediary that takes American AI infrastructure into the Gulf and the Global South and exports "portable sovereignty" (Digital Embassies); HUMAIN is a newer, top-down consolidation of Saudi state AI assets built around domestic scale. G42 has operating history and audited (subsidiary) revenue; HUMAIN has larger stated power targets and no disclosed revenue.

### Cited Findings
- One dated side-by-side (Lucidity Insights, 9 Nov 2026): G42 founded 2018, "built bottom-up", about ten operating companies, listed subsidiaries ~US$1.4bn 2025 revenue; HUMAIN launched by PIF May 2025, "built top-down", chaired by the Crown Prince, CEO Tareq Amin, Aramco agreed (Oct 2025) to take 49% and contributed US$2.6bn cash, US$3bn into xAI (Feb 2026), targets 1.9 GW by 2030 and 6.6 GW by 2034, no revenue disclosed, capped at 35,000 GB300-equivalents under case-by-case licences. Summary judgement: G42 "sells access", HUMAIN "sells scale"; G42 has "seven years of head start, audited revenue and a stronger compliance position", HUMAIN "greater ambition, capital momentum and home-market scale, but fewer confirmed deployments". (Secondary source; HUMAIN figures to be checked against the HUMAIN researcher's notes.) — [Lucidity Insights](https://lucidityinsights.com/articles/g42-vs-humain-inside-uae-ksa-ai-race)
- G42's stated priorities (board, Nov 2025): sovereign-grade compute/cloud, agentic AI, "AI-native nations", the "Intelligence Grid", deeper UAE–US collaboration. — [Abu Dhabi Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/technology/tahnoon-bin-zayed-chairs-g42-board-of-directors-meeting/)
- Export model: Digital Embassies + Greenshield (Jan 2026); overseas nodes financed partly by non-dilutive debt (HSBC) and local partners' consumption commitments (Vietnam). — [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/bybnd6z-g42-introduces-digital-embassies-greenshield-make); [Middle East AI News](https://www.middleeastainews.com/p/1b-usage-commitments-underpin-g42)
- Carnegie (June 2026) treats G42 as the UAE's vehicle for becoming "an AI infrastructure partner of choice", while arguing that sovereignty weakens when foreign actors own or govern the stack (search summary). — [Carnegie Endowment](https://carnegieendowment.org/research/2026/06/early-lessons-in-the-pursuit-of-sovereign-ai)
- Both groups were reported in Sept 2026 to be moving toward outside capital (G42: raise/US stake; HUMAIN: listing "preparation team") (search summary). — [AGBI](https://www.agbi.com/ai/2026/09/gulf-ai-giants-humain-and-g42-look-to-raise-outside-capital/)

### Inferences
- Structural differences for the comparison: (1) owner — ruling-family private vehicle (Royal Group) with US minority investors versus sovereign fund + national oil company; (2) US technology relationship — G42 embedded US shareholders, a US director, US-specified monitoring and licence-free status with a sunset; HUMAIN a licensed customer; (3) operating model — G42 a federation of separately branded companies, two listed; HUMAIN a single integrated company; (4) geography — G42 exporting (US, Europe, Asia, Africa attempts); HUMAIN primarily domestic build.
- G42's intermediary model makes it more exposed than HUMAIN to a change in US policy in either direction: it gains most from alignment and has the most to lose from the April 2027 condition.

### Gaps
- No tier-1 (FT/Bloomberg/think-tank) head-to-head comparison was retrieved; the one comparison used is a regional business publication.
