## 1. HUMAIN — company overview, ownership, leadership

### Takeaway
HUMAIN is a Riyadh-based, state-created AI company launched on 12 May 2025 under PIF; since 1 November 2026 it is owned 51% by PIF and 49% by Saudi Aramco (via a wholly owned subsidiary), after Aramco contributed about SAR 9.9bn (US$2.6bn) in cash plus AI/compute/digital assets. It is led by CEO Tareq Amin (ex-head of Aramco's tech arm) and reported as chaired by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman.

### Cited Findings
- Founded 12 May 2025; HQ Riyadh; announced at an investment forum in Riyadh; Wikipedia lists Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman as founder; CEO Tareq Amin. — [Wikipedia: Humain (rev. 3 Nov 2026)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humain) (S/A)
- "Humain was launched in May 2025 and is chaired by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman. The Public Investment Fund owns it." (22 Oct 2026) — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/10/22/saudi-arabias-humain-expands-nvidia-deal-with-84mw-ai-computing-plan/) (S)
- Nvidia's 13 May 2025 release describes HUMAIN as "a new subsidiary of Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund" covering the full AI value chain in four areas: next-generation data centres; infrastructure and cloud platforms; AI models (including an Arabic multimodal LLM); AI solutions. Serves public and private sector. — [Nvidia newsroom, 13 May 2025](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/humain-and-nvidia-announce-strategic-partnership-to-build-ai-factories-of-the-future-in-saudi-arabia) (P)
- **[MoU/NON-BINDING → later completed]** 28 Oct 2025: PIF and Aramco signed a non-binding term sheet for Aramco to take a "significant minority stake" with PIF keeping the majority; both would contribute "AI assets, capabilities, and talent"; subject to definitive agreements and regulatory approvals. — [Aramco press release, 28 Oct 2025](https://www.aramco.com/en/news-media/news/2025/pif-and-aramco-agree-for-aramco-to-acquire-a-significant-minority-stake-in-humain) (P); [PIF press release 2025](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/news-and-insights/press-releases/2025/pif-and-aramco-agree-for-aramco-to-acquire-a-significant-minority-stake-in-humain-with-pif-retaining-majority-ownership/) (P, not opened)
- **[ACTUAL]** Transaction closed 1 Nov 2026: Aramco (through a wholly owned subsidiary) holds 49%, PIF 51%. Consideration: approx. SAR 9.9bn (US$2.6bn) cash contributions from Aramco during 2026 "in addition to AI, compute, and digital assets". — [PIF press release, 1 Nov 2026](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/news-and-insights/press-releases/2026/pif-and-aramco-close-humain-transaction-to-accelerate-humain-global-growth/) (P, snippet; page returned 403 on direct fetch); corroborated by [W.Media](https://w.media/?p=45021) (S, snippet), [Wamda (Arabic)](https://www.wamda.com/ar/2026/11/saudi-aramco-acquires-49-humain-2-6-billion-cash-injection) (S, snippet), [AI Magazine](https://aimagazine.com/news/pif-aramco-humain-and-the-2-6bn-sovereign-ai-initiative) (S, snippet)
- Saudi Company for Artificial Intelligence (SCAI), previously wholly owned by PIF, "has already been transferred to HUMAIN" (stated Oct 2025). — [Aramco press release, 28 Oct 2025](https://www.aramco.com/en/news-media/news/2025/pif-and-aramco-agree-for-aramco-to-acquire-a-significant-minority-stake-in-humain) (P)
- Pre-announcement reporting said Aramco Digital and its Groq-based AI cluster were likely among assets to be merged, but "Aramco and PIF provided no details on what assets and talent would be transferred to HUMAIN, nor the timeframe". — [Middle East AI News](https://www.middleeastainews.com/p/aramco-to-merge-ai-assets-with-humain) (S, snippet)
- CEO Tareq Amin previously led Saudi Aramco's tech arm. — [Semafor, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.semafor.com/article/09/04/2026/the-switzerland-of-ai-saudi-arabias-big-ai-bet) (S)
- International footprint/ambition: HUMAIN plans offices in the US, France and the UK alongside Saudi Arabia in connection with a planned large global VC fund **[TARGET]**. — [Semafor, 3 Sept 2026](https://www.semafor.com/article/09/03/2026/saudi-ai-firm-humain-to-launch-giant-global-vc-fund-this-year) (S, snippet); [Semafor, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.semafor.com/article/09/04/2026/the-switzerland-of-ai-saudi-arabias-big-ai-bet) (S)
- IPO: Amin said in Oct 2025 he expected a dual listing in Saudi Arabia and on Nasdaq within three to four years (i.e. ~2029); in Sept 2026 he was recruiting finance/IPO-preparation staff; no banks appointed, no valuation, size or date announced **[TARGET]**. — [FWDstart, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.fwdstart.me/p/humain-starts-ipo-preparations-16-months-after-launch-with-revenues-still-undisclosed) (S)

### Inferences
- HUMAIN is a deliberately created state vehicle, not an organically grown private firm: it was stood up by PIF, absorbed an existing PIF AI company (SCAI), and has now been recapitalised by the state oil company. Both shareholders are state-controlled, so it is effectively 100% state-owned.
- The Aramco closing resolves, at shareholder level, some of the overlap between HUMAIN and Aramco Digital, but which Aramco assets actually moved is not publicly itemised in the sources retrieved.
- An implied post-money equity value cannot be derived from the US$2.6bn cash figure because the non-cash asset contributions on both sides are not valued publicly.

### Gaps
- Board composition beyond the chair; headcount; list of legal subsidiaries: not found.
- Whether Aramco Digital as an entity (and its Groq inference cluster) was transferred at closing: unconfirmed; closing releases reportedly do not name it.
- Valuation of HUMAIN: none published (FWDstart).
- Unverified from researcher's prior knowledge (not retrieved this session, treat as unconfirmed): that SDAIA's ALLaM model and related assets moved to HUMAIN via SCAI; that HUMAIN is formally headquartered in Riyadh's KAFD or similar.

## 2. HUMAIN — strategic mandate and government link

### Takeaway
HUMAIN's stated purpose is to make Saudi Arabia "an international AI powerhouse" with a full-stack offer (data centres, cloud, models, applications); it is tied explicitly to Vision 2030 and PIF's mandate to develop strategic sectors and localise technology. No published statute or decree giving HUMAIN a formal exclusive "sovereign mandate" was found; its mandate is expressed through ownership, the Crown Prince's chairmanship and PIF/Aramco statements.

### Cited Findings
- Mandate per Nvidia/HUMAIN release: position Saudi Arabia as "an international AI powerhouse", combining infrastructure, frontier models, digital platforms and human-capital development; MCIT minister Abdullah Alswaha quoted in the release. — [Nvidia newsroom, 13 May 2025](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/humain-and-nvidia-announce-strategic-partnership-to-build-ai-factories-of-the-future-in-saudi-arabia) (P)
- PIF/Aramco rationale: combine "key AI assets" under HUMAIN to position Saudi Arabia as a global AI hub; supports "PIF's broader mandate to develop strategic sectors and localize modern technologies"; lets Aramco apply HUMAIN infrastructure to industrial applications and capture "rising energy demand linked to AI growth" (Amin Nasser). — [Aramco press release, 28 Oct 2025](https://www.aramco.com/en/news-media/news/2025/pif-and-aramco-agree-for-aramco-to-acquire-a-significant-minority-stake-in-humain) (P)
- Launch timing: launched under PIF shortly before US President Trump's May 2025 visit to the Kingdom, when the first US chip partnerships were announced. — [Semafor, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.semafor.com/article/09/04/2026/the-switzerland-of-ai-saudi-arabias-big-ai-bet) (S); [The National, 22 Oct 2026](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/10/22/saudi-arabias-humain-expands-nvidia-deal-with-84mw-ai-computing-plan/) (S)
- Positioning: Amin wants HUMAIN to be a "Switzerland" for open-weight models between the US and China; Bloomberg reported HUMAIN built an AI platform on MiniMax's M3, a Chinese open-source model; partnerships with US firm Reflection AI and an announcement with Mistral. — [Semafor, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.semafor.com/article/09/04/2026/the-switzerland-of-ai-saudi-arabias-big-ai-bet) (S)
- Division of labour with SDAIA: secondary sites state that after May 2025 HUMAIN took over commercial deployment of ALLaM and related infrastructure while SDAIA retained regulatory authority over data governance and AI strategy. — [vision2030.ai analysis](https://vision2030.ai/sectors/technology/ai-strategy/) (A, snippet); not confirmed in an SDAIA or HUMAIN primary document.
- The stc–HUMAIN data-centre project is described by stc as aligned with Vision 2030 and the National Strategy for Data and AI. — [Fast Company Middle East](https://fastcompanyme.com/news/stc-and-humain-extend-deal-to-build-up-to-1gw-ai-data-centers-in-saudi-arabia/) (S)

### Inferences
- The mandate is political and ownership-based rather than statutory: HUMAIN is the state's chosen commercial AI vehicle, with SDAIA as regulator/strategist and MCIT as sector ministry. This is consistent with (but not proven to be) a de facto national-champion designation.
- Use of a Chinese open-weight model alongside US chip dependence is a notable tension for a company whose hardware supply relies on US export approvals.

### Gaps
- No royal decree, Council of Ministers decision or published charter granting HUMAIN an exclusive sovereign AI mandate was found.
- Whether government entities are required or merely encouraged to buy from HUMAIN: not found.

## 3. HUMAIN — business model, products, partnerships

### Takeaway
HUMAIN sells (i) build-to-suit data-centre capacity to hyperscalers and AI labs who bring their own GPUs, (ii) its own GPU cloud ("HUMAIN Cloud") and an inference API marketplace, (iii) Arabic-first models and apps (ALLaM, HUMAIN Chat, HUMAIN ONE), and (iv) acts as an investor (xAI, Luma AI, planned VC fund). Named customers are few and several are companies HUMAIN has invested in.

### Cited Findings
- Business lines per CEO: build-to-suit data centres for hyperscalers "which buy the GPUs"; an AI inference API marketplace for selling frontier models. — [Semafor, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.semafor.com/article/09/04/2026/the-switzerland-of-ai-saudi-arabias-big-ai-bet) (S)
- HUMAIN Cloud customers are described as "AI companies, government entities and businesses". — [The National, 22 Oct 2026](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/10/22/saudi-arabias-humain-expands-nvidia-deal-with-84mw-ai-computing-plan/) (S)
- Named customer **[ACTUAL]**: the Saudi Ministry of Commerce is among the first customers of the Nvidia-powered AI cloud. — [FWDstart, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.fwdstart.me/p/humain-starts-ipo-preparations-16-months-after-launch-with-revenues-still-undisclosed) (S)
- Luma AI: HUMAIN led Luma's US$900m Series C in 2025 **[ACTUAL investment]**; Luma contracted for the entire initial 100MW of the AMD–Cisco–HUMAIN venture **[COMMITTED per source]** and is to be a customer of "Project Halo". — [FWDstart, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.fwdstart.me/p/humain-starts-ipo-preparations-16-months-after-launch-with-revenues-still-undisclosed) (S); first-customer status also per Reuters via [DCD](https://datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/amd-cisco-and-humain-set-up-jv-for-1gw-of-ai-infrastructure-in-saudi-arabia/) (S, snippet)
- Together AI: agreed (early Sept 2026) to use 250MW; the companies "expect" more than US$5bn gross annualised revenue in the first operating year **[TARGET/projection; revenue split and contracted share undisclosed]**. — [FWDstart, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.fwdstart.me/p/humain-starts-ipo-preparations-16-months-after-launch-with-revenues-still-undisclosed) (S)
- Microsoft: HUMAIN ONE bundled with Microsoft 365 Copilot, targeting one million users across the Middle East and Africa **[TARGET; terms undisclosed]**. — [FWDstart, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.fwdstart.me/p/humain-starts-ipo-preparations-16-months-after-launch-with-revenues-still-undisclosed) (S)
- xAI: HUMAIN invested US$3bn in xAI **[ACTUAL per Semafor]**; xAI has since been folded into SpaceX ahead of its IPO; Amin called it "a home run". — [Semafor, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.semafor.com/article/09/04/2026/the-switzerland-of-ai-saudi-arabias-big-ai-bet) (S)
- Unnamed US customer: 50MW of Vera Rubin capacity targeted for June 2027 **[MoU/NON-BINDING: "subject to final agreements"]**. — [The National, 22 Oct 2026](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/10/22/saudi-arabias-humain-expands-nvidia-deal-with-84mw-ai-computing-plan/) (S)
- Nebius: preliminary framework agreement signed 21 Oct 2026 with HUMAIN and DataVolt as prospective anchor customer at Oxagon (one report: up to 50MW) **[MoU/NON-BINDING]**. — [The Technology Express](https://thetechnologyexpress.com/humain-datavolt-and-nebius-advance-neom-ai-campus/) (S, snippet)
- Products: HUMAIN Chat — free Arabic-first chat app (iOS, Android, web) powered by ALLaM; ALLaM 34B reportedly trained on 500bn+ Arabic tokens (Arab News, 5 Sept 2025); an operating system built on ALLaM was reported Oct 2025. — [Wikipedia: Humain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humain) (S/A), citing [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2614195/saudi-arabia)
- December 2026 rollout **[TARGET]**: HUMAIN "will make its computing power, AI models and software available to developers and businesses" as a bundle; training programme with hands-on labs in Riyadh early 2027. A search summary gave the date as 15 December and included government agencies. — [The Circuit, 13 Nov 2026](http://circuit.news/2026/11/13/pifs-humain-sets-up-december-rollout-for-its-saudi-ai-tools/) (S, article truncated)
- Investment vehicles **[TARGET]**: planned US$10bn global VC fund and a Saudi-focused vehicle "HUMAIN Limitless". — [FWDstart, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.fwdstart.me/p/humain-starts-ipo-preparations-16-months-after-launch-with-revenues-still-undisclosed) (S); [Semafor, 3 Sept 2026](https://www.semafor.com/article/09/03/2026/saudi-ai-firm-humain-to-launch-giant-global-vc-fund-this-year) (S, snippet; Semafor did not state the US$10bn figure)

### Inferences
- Customer concentration and circularity: Luma AI and xAI are both investees and (prospective) customers; FWDstart explicitly flags that some of the clearest external demand comes from companies HUMAIN funded.
- The build-to-suit model shifts GPU capex and export-licence burden to the tenant, lowering HUMAIN's capital intensity but making it closer to a data-centre developer than a cloud/AI company for that part of the business.
- Government (Ministry of Commerce) is the only named domestic customer in the sources retrieved; private Saudi enterprise demand is not evidenced.

### Gaps
- No customer or geographic revenue breakdown exists publicly (FWDstart).
- HUMAIN Chat user numbers; HUMAIN ONE adoption; details of "Project Halo"; the reported HUMAIN "Horizon Pro" AI PC: not found this session.
- Terms of the Reflection AI and Mistral arrangements: not found.

## 4. HUMAIN — technology, infrastructure, chip and hyperscaler deals

### Takeaway
Announced capacity is in gigawatts (3GW by 2030; >6GW by 2034) but verified operational capacity is very small: the only disclosed figure is 1.1MW at launch of the AI cloud. Chip supply is entirely third-party (Nvidia, AMD, Qualcomm, AWS Trainium, Groq) and depends on US export approvals; the model layer (ALLaM) is in-house but small-scale.

An older analysis cites 1.9GW by 2030 — [vision2030.ai](https://vision2030.ai/analysis/humain/) (A, snippet); treat 3GW/6GW as the current public targets.

- **[ACTUAL, unquantified]** AMD and Cisco said infrastructure built with HUMAIN is live and serving customers; no MW, revenue or pricing disclosed. — [FWDstart, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.fwdstart.me/p/humain-starts-ipo-preparations-16-months-after-launch-with-revenues-still-undisclosed) (S)
- First two sites (Riyadh and Dammam): construction announced Aug 2025, each up to 100MW initially, originally due to be operational in Q2 2026 (Bloomberg, 25 Aug 2025). — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-25/saudi-s-humain-to-open-data-centers-with-us-chips-in-early-2026) (S, snippet). - The separate programmes (Nvidia 500MW, AMD/Cisco 1GW, xAI 500MW, stc JV 1GW, Oxagon 100MW, Together AI 250MW, AWS 50MW, Qualcomm 200MW) overlap in unknown ways and should not be summed; several likely sit on the same campuses.
- Technology is predominantly third-party; proprietary elements are ALLaM, HUMAIN Chat/ONE and the integration layer.

### Gaps
- Total MW energised by HUMAIN as of November 2026: undisclosed.
- Number of GPUs actually delivered: HUMAIN declined to disclose (July 2026, per aggregator).

- Oracle or Google Cloud partnerships with HUMAIN specifically: none found. The Google Cloud–PIF AI hub (reported ~US$10bn, announced 2024–25) could not be researched; status unknown.
- Primary releases from AWS, AMD, Qualcomm and Cisco were not opened; figures above for those come from secondary snippets.

## 5. HUMAIN — financials

### Takeaway
HUMAIN publishes no revenue, profit or valuation. The only hard financial facts are Aramco's US$2.6bn cash contribution (2026), a Blackstone US$3bn commitment, an up-to-US$1.2bn infrastructure financing (January 2026), and investments in xAI (US$3bn) and Luma AI. Everything else is target or projection.

- **[ACTUAL — non-disclosure]** "HUMAIN has not disclosed revenue, EBITDA, or profit", nor any breakdown by customer or geography, despite tens of billions of dollars of announced plans. — [FWDstart, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.fwdstart.me/p/humain-starts-ipo-preparations-16-months-after-launch-with-revenues-still-undisclosed) (S)

## 10. National ecosystem: strategy, regulation, institutions, incentives

### Takeaway
Saudi Arabia runs a state-led model: NSDAI (2020, SDAIA) sets AI targets; a Cloud First Policy pushes public-sector demand to cloud; CST registers and regulates cloud providers and requires government data to stay in-Kingdom; PDPL (enforced since Sept 2024) governs personal data; a Cloud Computing Special Economic Zone offers a hosting regime for hyperscalers. PIF is the common owner behind HUMAIN, stc and (via Aramco/state) most domestic capacity.

### Cited Findings
- NSDAI: unveiled October 2020 at the Global AI Summit in Riyadh, led by SDAIA; targets include training up to 20,000 data and AI specialists and 300 active startups by 2030. — [The National](https://thenational-the-national-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/business/technology/saudi-arabia-unveils-national-strategy-for-data-and-ai-1.1097271) (S, snippet); [Asharq Al-Awsat](https://english.aawsat.com/home/article/2577621/saudi-arabia-outlines-strategy-become-global-leader-ai-2030) (S, snippet); [Saudipedia](https://saudipedia.com/en/national-strategy-for-data-and-ai-nsdai) (S, not opened)

### Inferences
- Demand mechanism: Cloud First Policy plus in-Kingdom residency for government data creates captive public-sector demand that must be served from locally registered infrastructure — structurally favouring center3 (as host) and any provider with a local region, including foreign hyperscalers.
- Origin of champions: HUMAIN = deliberately created (2025) and consolidated by the state; stc = legacy state incumbent, partially floated, deliberately repositioned (center3 carve-out, TAWAL sale to PIF) toward digital infrastructure. Neither is an organically private champion.
- Institutional layering: strategy/regulation (SDAIA, MCIT, CST, DGA) is separated from commercial execution (HUMAIN, stc), with PIF as owner of both executors.

## 11. Critical assessment: risks, sceptical views, gaps

### Takeaway
The main credibility gap is between gigawatt-scale announcements and disclosed delivery (1.1MW live plus 14MW due from Q4 2026 on the Nvidia track; no revenue disclosed). Other risks: US export-licence dependence, power/grid build-out, demand that is partly circular or state-linked, thin frontier-model capability, and overlapping state vehicles. Independent sceptical analysis in tier-one outlets was sparse in what could be retrieved; much of the critical commentary comes from lower-reliability sites.

### Cited Findings
- Delivery vs announcements: "much of it is still under construction or at an early stage"; gigawatt announcements alone will not support an IPO case; investors will want revenue, margins, capital intensity, customer concentration and the share of non-government, non-PIF customers. — [FWDstart, 7 Sept 2026](https://www.fwdstart.me/p/humain-starts-ipo-preparations-16-months-after-launch-with-revenues-still-undisclosed) (S)

### Inferences
- Overlapping mandates: before Nov 2026, HUMAIN, SDAIA (ALLaM/NCAI), Aramco Digital, stc/center3, SCCC and DataVolt all had AI-infrastructure roles. The Aramco stake and SCAI transfer consolidate some of this under HUMAIN, and the pending center3 JV would align stc; SDAIA remains regulator and strategist. Residual overlap persists between HUMAIN Cloud and the stc/Oracle Alloy/SCCC sovereign-cloud offers for the same government customers.
- Dual technology alignment (US chips at HUMAIN; Chinese stack at SCCC; MiniMax-based platform reported at HUMAIN) may complicate future US export approvals — an inference, not a reported regulatory finding.
- For stc/center3 the risk profile is different: steady cash generation but flat operating profit, and dependence on HUMAIN/hyperscaler anchor tenants to justify a US$10bn build.
- Talent: evidence of training pledges only; no data on actual AI workforce size was retrieved, so talent remains an unquantified risk.
