## 1. G42 group as UAE National AI Champion (incl. Inception, M42, Presight, Space42, MGX)

### Takeaway
G42 (Group 42 Holding Ltd, Abu Dhabi, founded 2018) is the clearest state-linked national AI champion in the Gulf: chaired and controlled by Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed (UAE National Security Adviser), with Mubadala, Silver Lake (~$800m, 2021) and Microsoft ($1.5bn, 2024) as minority investors, and with exact shareholdings undisclosed. In 2026 its position was strengthened by US licence-free chip access (July 2026, time-limited to 6 April 2027) but challenged by the Iran war (Stargate UAE named as a target; campus plan reportedly being dispersed) and by reported pressure to become US-majority-owned to keep chip access.

### Inferences
- G42 is a government-created champion, not an organic one: its chair is the national security adviser, who also chairs MGX and the Abu Dhabi AI council (AIATC); its investors include a state fund; and its major customers are state entities. The concentration of chair roles in one individual across G42, MGX and the policy council is the central governance-concentration risk.
- MGX's headline numbers mix three different things: (a) a closed US$49bn fund (committed capital), (b) consortium deal values such as the US$40bn Aligned acquisition (not MGX's cheque), and (c) pledges such as the EUR 30–50bn France campus. Only (a) and the confirmed participations in funding rounds are firm; MGX's actual deployed capital per deal is largely undisclosed.
- The group's only audited public financials are Presight and Space42. Presight shows real, growing, increasingly international revenue (38.5% non-UAE) — but its foreign customers are overwhelmingly other governments (Kazakhstan, Jordan, Albania), so "international" does not mean "private-sector". Space42 revenue declined in 2025.
- The reported consideration of US majority ownership (Sept 2026) suggests that the "sovereign national champion" identity is in tension with dependence on US chips: the July 2026 BIS authorisation reportedly requires G42/Core42 to become US companies or seek fresh authorisation by 6 April 2027.

## 2. Core42 + Khazna Data Centers as UAE National Cloud Champion (sovereign cloud, Stargate UAE, 5GW campus)

### Takeaway
Core42 (cloud/compute operator) and Khazna (data-centre builder/owner; G42 majority, MGX and Silver Lake minorities since March 2025) together form the UAE's de facto national cloud champion, anchored by Abu Dhabi government demand and Microsoft/OpenAI/Oracle tenancy. As of mid-November 2026 the first 200MW of Stargate UAE has **no published commissioning notice** (target was 2026 / Q3 2026; two 100MW buildings enclosed by Aug 2026), and the 5GW campus is reportedly being redesigned into dispersed, hardened clusters after Iranian threats.

### Inferences
- Honest assessment: Bahrain never had a national champion; it deliberately chose to be an early adopter and regional host for a foreign hyperscaler. The destruction of the AWS region removes the centrepiece of that strategy and exposes the concentration risk of a single-hyperscaler, single-country residency model. Beyon's Data Oasis is the only visible domestic alternative but is small-scale colocation, not a sovereign AI cloud.
- The Bahrain case is the strongest empirical counter-argument in the region to "in-country = resilient" sovereign-cloud narratives and will likely push GCC regulators towards cross-border replication arrangements (cf. G42's "Digital Embassies" concept).
